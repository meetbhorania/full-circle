# Brew & Co refund policy

Attach to brew-team as a document. Instructions for agents: "The refund rules. Read this before proposing any refund or credit. Never go beyond it."

1. **Duplicate charges** (the same order charged more than once): refund every duplicate in full. Keep the first charge.
2. **Goodwill credit:** a customer affected by a fault on our side gets a £5 credit on their next order.
3. **Who approves:**
   - Any refund or credit needs a person from Finance to reply "approved" in the thread.
   - Refunds over £50 also need the engineering lead.
4. **Never** refund a charge that was not duplicated or faulty without a person telling you to.
5. **Record it:** state the charge ID, the amount and who approved it, and post a "log:" line in the thread.
