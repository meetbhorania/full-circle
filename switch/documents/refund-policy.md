# Brew & Co refund policy

Attach to brew-team as a document. Instructions for agents: "The refund rules. Read this before proposing any refund or credit. Never go beyond it."

1. **Duplicate charges** (the same order charged more than once): refund every duplicate in full and keep the first charge, **for every customer the fault affected**, whether or not they complained.
2. **Goodwill credit:** every customer affected by a fault on our side gets a £5 credit on their next order (POST /api/credits).
3. **Who approves:**
   - Any refund or credit needs a person from Finance to reply "approved (Finance)" in the thread.
   - If the refunds total more than £50, the engineering lead must also reply "approved (engineering lead)" in a separate message.
4. **Never** refund a charge that was not duplicated or faulty without a person telling you to.
5. **Record it:** state the charge ID, the amount and who approved it, and post a "log:" line in the thread.
